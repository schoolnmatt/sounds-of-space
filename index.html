Sounds of Space

While planets and other extraterrestrial bodies are not able to emit traditional sound due to the vacuum of outer space, we can still observe and enjoy extraterrestrial sounds from converted optical, infared, and x-ray data. These converted sounds are known as sonifications.

Can you match the sonifications to their correct source in outer space?

Instructions

Start the game and click the audio play button to listen to the sonification.

Listen closely, and try to match the audio to the object in space it comes from.
